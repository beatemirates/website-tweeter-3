About us

Built for fleets.
Ready nationwide.

We specialize in commercial fleets, school buses, heavy-duty equipment, service vehicles, and specialized transportation assets.

Our technicians install, remove, replace, troubleshoot, and quality-check GPS systems, vehicle gateways, electronic logging equipment, cameras, DVRs, driver-monitoring equipment, antennas, sensors, wiring harnesses, and other vehicle electronics.

We mobilize experienced technicians and field leaders throughout the country—from small groups of vehicles at one location to thousands of assets across multiple states. Our work is built around dependable workmanship, accurate installation, thorough documentation, safe execution, and consistent communication.
